# Rullo Design Rules

Numbers first, a warm light page, and every color has one job. Built to be read from the saddle, a few feet from the screen.

Source: [Rullo Design System](https://claude.ai/artifact/MwUnE5KEPgbmD8FAaiLfcs) (made when the app was called Weem Watts; it still shows the old name and the old Cobalt accent). In code, `src/styles/tokens.css` is the single source of truth. No hex color may appear anywhere else, with two exceptions: Strava's button image (below) and the app icons (see "Logo and icons").

## Principles

- **One big number.** Live power is the hero. Everything else supports it.
- **Color means something.** Chrome is neutral warm gray. Color appears only for an action, a channel, a state, or a power zone. Live power is in the color of its zone (see Power zones), and the zone's code is always beside it.
- **Olive is rare.** `accent` has two jobs: the primary action (one per screen: Connect, then Start ride / Start workout in the same slot once connected, except while the pairing step is open, when its Show device list button is, or the watch card's Connect, and on the Workout page, where the step button is primary, in the workout builder, where Save is primary, and on Home, where the next-step button is primary, and on a ride summary, where Download FIT is primary; the top bar's buttons are secondary on all four, except on the finish screen, the summary of the ride that just ended, where the page has no primary and the top bar keeps the ended ride's: Upload to Strava when it is on offer, else Download FIT) and route progress (the ridden stretch and position dot on the elevation profile). It never fills anything bigger than a button. Live power was Olive's third job until Nov 24, 2026. It is in the color of its zone now. Olive comes back for the Power number and its trace only when there is no zone to show: with `POWER_ZONE_COLOR` off in `src/config.ts`.
- **Calm while riding.** Numbers update in place. Nothing jumps, bounces, or flashes. What does move is listed under Motion: a state change, a discrete event, or a position that follows the ride.
- **Heart rate is a number, not a channel.** It has its own tile in `fg`, with no zones, no zone color and no target. A stale or lost sensor turns the tile `fg-subtle` and changes nothing else: the StatusPill, the ride and the trainer are about the trainer only.
- **Never show frozen numbers as live.** Stale data turns `fg-subtle` and the StatusPill says "Reconnecting". An auto-paused ride does the same with "Paused" (`idle` tone), and the control row status reads "Paused. Pedal to resume."
- **Say when nothing is being saved.** Nothing is recorded until the rider presses Start. Connected and waiting, the screen says so in three calm places and nothing flashes: the StatusPill reads "Not recording" (`warn`), the Time tile's label row reads "Not recording" (`ww-label` in `state-under`), and the control row's second line reads "Press Start ride." (or "Press Start workout."). Time and Distance sit at 0:00 and 0.00 in `fg-subtle`. Power, cadence and speed are live.
- **Say who sets the load.** The control row's first line is always one of "Workout sets the load.", "Hills set the load.", "You set the load.", "You set the load for now." (a workout is loaded and not started) or "Workout is done." It follows what the trainer is doing, so it changes when the rider switches mode by hand.
- **Units are lb and miles.** Never show kg or km.

## The Strava exception

Strava's brand guidelines (read Nov 20, 2026; last revised Sep 29, 2025) set how an app may show its name and marks. This is the one place the rules above give way, and it is kept as small as those guidelines allow:

- **Connect with Strava** on the Profile tab is Strava's own button image, the white one (`public/strava/btn_strava_connect_with_white.svg`), at its own size (237 x 48) and never recolored, cropped or restyled. It contains Strava's orange, the only color in the app that is not a token; the hex lives inside Strava's file, not in our CSS. The white button has no edge, so on a white panel it sits in a 1px `line-strong` frame with a 6px radius (`.ww-strava-connect`). The frame is ours; the image is untouched. The white button was chosen over the orange one so the screen keeps one loud color.
- Everything else about Strava uses this system: **Upload to Strava** is an ordinary `Button`, and the link to the activity reads exactly **View on Strava**, bold and underlined in `fg` (the guidelines accept bold, underline or orange; orange is not used).
- The Profile block ends with "Compatible with Strava" in mono `fg-subtle`, the wording the guidelines give. Strava's name is never larger than the text around it and never part of this app's name.

## Logo and icons

The name is always "Rullo", title case. The descriptor is "Free indoor rides, right in your browser."

The wordmark is a heavy italic serif "Rullo" with a tall double L and a round "o". It comes in two versions, both SVG in `public/brand/`. Never use a raster copy.

| File | What it is | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rullo-wordmark.svg` | The full version, with thin inline cuts in the letters | 48px tall or larger |
| `rullo-wordmark-solid.svg` | The same letters with the cuts filled in | Under 48px tall, including the top bar |

- **The 48px rule.** Under 48px the inline cuts close up and blur, so the solid version is the only one allowed there.
- Both files have no background, no fixed width or height (they scale from the viewBox, 1583 by 511) and `currentColor` fills. Color comes from CSS: `.ww-wordmark` draws the solid file as a mask over `fg`. Give it `role="img"` and `aria-label="Rullo"`.
- The wordmark is in one place, the top bar. Do not add it elsewhere without asking.
- `public/brand/rullo-r.svg` is the R on its own, the letter the app icons are made from, also with a `currentColor` fill. It is not used in the app.
- **App icons** in `public/` (`favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png`, `icon-maskable-512.png`) are finished artwork: a cream R on an olive square. They are never redrawn or recolored, and like Strava's button they carry their own hex values. The `theme-color` in `index.html` and the colors in `manifest.webmanifest` repeat `bg` because neither can read a CSS token.

The screen still reads because each of these has its own place and its own words. A state color is on the StatusPill's dot or a cue, next to its label. Olive is on a button or the course profile. A zone color is on the Power number and its trace only, with the zone chip's code (Z1 to Z7) in the same tile. A stale number is `fg-subtle`, the chip is hidden and the pill says "Reconnecting" or "Paused". So a red Z6 number is not an error and a gray Z1 number is not stale, and the code and the pill say so.

### Power zones

For workout blocks, zone codes (v3.2) and live power (Nov 24, 2026). Each meets 3:1 on `surface`; the table has the numbers. Z2 is gray-blue, not blue, so it never reads as the accent. Always pair a zone color with its code (Z1 to Z7). The boundaries live in one list, `ZONES` in `src/lib/workout.ts` (tops at 55, 75, 90, 105, 120 and 150% FTP). The workout strip, the builder graph and `ZoneTable` are all built from it. A block is drawn in the zone of the whole watts it comes to at the rider's FTP, so a block's color and the chart's watt ranges always agree.

The **zone chip** on the Power tile shows the zone of the power on screen: `zoneAt()` with the smoothed power and the profile FTP, so it agrees with `ZoneTable` and the strip.

**Zone color on live power.** Nate asked for this on Nov 24, 2026, so the zone can be read from the saddle on the biggest thing on the screen. It replaces the old rule that power stays Olive and the number is never tinted.

- **The Power number** is in the token of the zone its chip shows (`z1` to `z7`). It is the same held zone (`ZONE_HOLD_S`), so the number and the chip change together and never flicker at an edge.
- **The power trace** is colored a second at a time: each stretch of the line is in the zone of the power at the second it ends on. Same `zoneAt()`, same FTP, no hold, because it is history.
- **Only the number's text and the line.** The tile's background, the label, the unit ("W" stays `fg-muted`) and every other power number (the workout row's Target, the ERG target, Home, the ride summary) are not tinted.
- **Color is never the only signal.** The chip and its code stay where they are.
- **Stale, paused or empty,** the number and the line are `fg-subtle`, as before.
- **Contrast.** The number and the 1.5px line are live graphics, so they need 3:1 on `surface`. All seven pass: Z1 3.21, Z2 3.76, Z3 3.57, Z4 3.32, Z5 3.58, Z6 5.21, Z7 6.37. Z1 and Z4 are the closest to the limit. A zone token must not get lighter without checking these.
- **Over the target band** the line would sit on `raised`, where four zones are under 3:1 (Z1 2.68, Z3 2.98, Z4 2.77, Z5 2.98; Z2 3.14, Z6 4.35, Z7 5.31). So a zone-colored line has a 1px `surface` hairline on each side, the two-tone rule below. The line is then always on `surface`.
- **One switch.** `POWER_ZONE_COLOR` in `src/config.ts`. Off, the number and the line are Olive again (8.2:1) and there is no hairline. It is there because this is a matter of taste Nate may reverse. See `docs/zone-color.md`.

An outline on a zone block is always two-tone: an `fg` line with a 1px `surface` hairline on each side. The `fg` line then sits on `surface` (17.4:1) whatever the fill, and the hairline is 3.2:1 or better against every zone (Z1 3.2, Z2 3.8, Z3 3.6, Z4 3.3, Z5 3.6, Z6 5.2, Z7 6.4). `fg` alone is not enough: it is 2.7:1 on `z7`. Used for the hovered block of `WorkoutStrip` (1px line), its heads-up block (the same 1px line, see Motion) and the selected block of `BuilderGraph` (2px line). The position line on `WorkoutStrip` follows the same rule with `accent` in place of `fg`, and the power trace's line with its zone token in place of `fg`.

## Type

- **Archivo** (variable, with the width axis) for everything. JetBrains Mono for small technical text only (smoothing toggle, footer).
- Installed through `@fontsource-variable`. No CDN.
- **Live numbers:** weight 600, `font-stretch: 112%`, `tabular-nums`, line-height 0.9 (`.ww-num`).
  - Power: 144px in the token of the zone shown, `z1` to `z7` (96px under 1200px wide). In `accent` with zone color off. See Power zones.
  - Other tiles: 56 to 64px in `fg`.
  - Control values (resistance level): 40px in `fg` (`.ww-num-control`).
  - Course stats (grade, elevation, climbed, to go): 28px in `fg` (`.ww-num-sm`).
  - Units: 40% of the number size, `fg-muted`, 4px after the number (`.ww-unit`).
- **Labels:** 12px, weight 500, uppercase, letter-spacing 0.06em, `fg-muted` (`.ww-label`).
- Never Inter or Geist.

## Spacing and shape

- Spacing steps only: 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48 / 64 (Tailwind `1 2 3 4 6 8 12 16`). Other steps are removed from the theme.
- Radius: 4 for chips and pills (`rounded-sm`), 6 for buttons (`rounded-md`), 10 for panels (`rounded-lg`). Fully round only for status dots.
- Depth comes from surface steps (white `surface` panels on the `bg` page, `raised` for controls) and 1px hairlines. No shadows, with one exception Nate made on Nov 24, 2026: **the pop-up card** (`.ww-dialog`) has `--ww-dialog-shadow`, six soft layers of growing size (1 to 32px), each `fg` at 7%, so it is tinted warm and never pure black. It is for a card that floats over the page under a scrim, and nothing else: panels, buttons, tiles, chips and overlays stay flat.

## Motion

The Ride screen moves in three cases only: a state changes, a discrete event happens, or a position follows the ride. Everything else holds still. Built in Motion Phase 1 (Nov 21, 2026). Phase 2 (Nov 22, 2026) added layout moves on the setup flow, under "Layout moves" below. `docs/motion.md` has what was built, what was left alone and what comes next.

**Rules**

- **Transform and opacity only.** Two exceptions, both asked for: a width that eases when its content changes (the StatusPill, the sliding chip) and a color that eases on a state change (stale gray-out, the status dot, the zone swatch, the Power number's zone color). Nothing that animates may move its neighbors on the Ride tab.
- **Ease is `--ww-ease`**, `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)`. Continuous movement is linear. Breathing is ease-in-out. No bounce, no overshoot, and nothing from Banned.
- **Live numbers never animate.** Power, cadence, heart rate, speed, distance and time update in place. No digit rolling, no tweening between values. The one thing about a number that may ease is its color, on a state change: the stale gray-out, and the Power number's zone color.
- **Never show motion the data does not back.** A position eases only up to a reading that has arrived. Stale or paused, a glide finishes at the last real position and stops. Nothing is extrapolated.
- **Never faster than the ride.** React draws on the ride snapshot (`TICK_MS`, 250ms, in `src/lib/ride.ts`) and on a reading. Motion between two draws belongs to CSS. No animation loop in JavaScript and no new dependency.
- **`prefers-reduced-motion`: everything snaps and nothing loops.** The `@media` block in `tokens.css` sets every duration to 0 and turns off the press scale, breathing, every enter and fade, the glide and the sliding chip.

**Tokens** (`tokens.css`)

| Token | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--ww-dur-fast` | 120ms | Hover and press (press scales to 0.98, `.ww-press`), the pill's label crossfade, the stepper cue, a hover overlay |
| `--ww-dur-base` | 200ms | State changes: stale gray-out, status dot, zone swatch, the Power number's zone color, pill width, sliding chip, every enter, the heads-up outline |
| `--ww-dur-glide` | 250ms | One ride snapshot. Must equal `TICK_MS`. The shortest a glide can be |
| `--ww-dur-breathe` | 1600ms | One breath of the status dot |
| `--ww-enter-shift` | 4px | How far a value travels on a first reading or a step |
| `--ww-enter-shift-interval` | 6px | The same, for the workout row at an interval change |
| `--ww-dur-layout` | 320ms | A layout move: something on the setup flow changes place or size |
| `--ww-dur-draw` | 480ms | A chart is drawn in (a course profile on the Workout tab, the bars on Home) |
| `--ww-step-shift` | 24px | How far a step of the setup flow slides |

**The moves**

- **Breathe** (`.ww-breathe`). The StatusPill's dot goes 1 to 0.35 to 1 in opacity over `--ww-dur-breathe`, ease-in-out, while something is being tried: Connecting, Reconnecting (with or without its attempt count). It is the only loop in the app. "Not recording" is a steady `warn` dot: nothing flashes there.
- **Pill label.** When the pill's words change, its width eases over `--ww-dur-base` and the label crossfades over `--ww-dur-fast`, so the top bar does not snap. A counter inside a label ("Reconnecting 2/30") updates in place.
- **Enter** (`.ww-enter`). A value arrives once: opacity 0 to 1 and a rise of `--ww-enter-shift`, over `--ww-dur-base`. It plays when the element is mounted for an event, never on a later update. Five uses:
  - **First data.** Power, Cadence and Speed enter when "--" becomes a reading (after Connect, or after a lost trainer comes back), 0 / 40 / 80ms apart. Heart rate enters on its own first bpm. A stale number stays on screen in `fg-subtle` and comes back by color alone, so it does not enter again.
  - **Interval change.** When the workout's interval changes while it is running (Skip interval too), the Interval number, Target with its unit, Cadence target and Next enter with a 6px rise. Not on the first render, on Start, on resume or when a workout is loaded. Interval left and Workout left are clocks and never animate.
  - **Watch card.** Its three steps enter 80ms apart, after the card itself has started to open. Once.
  - **Finish screen.** When Confirm end lands on the ride's summary, the "New best" lines and then the stats enter 40ms apart, left to right. Once, on arrival only: not when the summary is opened from Home, from the Ride tab's Ride summary button, with Back or Forward, after a reload, or on the way back from Strava's sign-in.
  - **Stepper cue.** A stepped value enters from 4px below on an increase and from above on a decrease, over `--ww-dur-fast`, starting at 40% opacity so the number stays readable while a key is held. Not on a mode switch, not on the first render, not when disabled.
- **Zone chip and zone color.** The swatch color and the Power number's color ease over `--ww-dur-base`, together. The zone shown changes only after power has been out of it for `ZONE_HOLD_S` (2 s), so neither flickers at an edge; on a steady ramp they catch up every 2 s. The digits do not take part: they change in place while the color eases.
- **Glide** (`.ww-glide`, `useGlide`). The workout strip's position line and wash, and the course dot, its drop line and the ridden stretch, are placed with a transform and eased linearly. A position changes when a reading arrives, about once a second from a trainer, not on every snapshot. So a glide takes as long as the last gap between two changes: never less than `--ww-dur-glide` and never more than `GLIDE_MAX_MS` (1200ms, `config.ts`). The mark reaches each reading about when the next one lands, which is what makes it continuous. It snaps on first paint, on a resize, on another course or workout, on Skip interval, on resume, on a jump of more than 2% of the width and on any backwards move. It never glides backwards.
- **Heads-up.** In the last `INTERVAL_HEADS_UP_S` (5 s) of an interval the next block in the strip gets the two-tone outline (under Power zones), fading in over `--ww-dur-base`. Skipped when the next interval is shorter than 5 s or the workout is done. The hover outline wins: while a block is pointed at, it is the only one outlined. No sound, and no color change on Interval left.
- **Sliding chip** (`.ww-chip-indicator`, `useSlidingChip`). The selected chip of `SegmentedControl` and `TabNav` is one element behind the options that moves to the selected one: transform and width over `--ww-dur-base`. Identical at rest. It snaps on first paint and on resize.
- **Pop-up card** (`.ww-dialog`). The watch card rises `--ww-dialog-shift` (12px) and grows from `--ww-dialog-scale` (0.98) while it fades in, over `--ww-dur-layout` with `--ww-ease`; the scrim fades in over `--ww-dur-base`. It closes at once: the browser's device list is next. A link row's arrow moves `--ww-nudge` (4px) on hover or focus, over `--ww-dur-fast`. It is drawn over the Ride tab and moves nothing on it.
- **Power trace.** It does not animate. The line is drawn again when a reading arrives, about once a second, and holds still between readings: no glide, no slide, no draw-in. With no readings (a dropout, a pause) it does not move at all. Its color eases to `fg-subtle` and back over `--ww-dur-base` with the number's (the stale gray-out); nothing else about it eases. A second's zone color is set when the second is drawn and never eases.
- **Hover overlay.** The workout strip's details fade in over `--ww-dur-fast` and are positioned over the page, so showing them moves and resizes nothing.
- **Chart cursor** (`.ww-scrub-line`, `.ww-scrub-dot`). On the ride summary's charts and the course overview's profile, the cursor line and its dots are placed with a transform and never eased: they are where the pointer is, or on the second a key stepped to. No transition, no glide. Only the chip fades in, once, over `--ww-dur-fast` (the time chip on the summary, the mile chip on the course); while the cursor moves it stays up and its numbers change in place. Showing or clearing the cursor moves and resizes nothing. Never on the Ride tab.
- **Finish draw-in.** On arrival at the finish screen the summary's charts are uncovered left to right over `--ww-dur-draw`, all rows under one cover (`.ww-draw-in`, see Draw-in under Layout moves; here it is a plain animation, not a layout move). Once, with the stats' enter. The page changes at once: there is no layout move from the Ride tab to the summary. Nothing else on the finish screen moves: no count-up, no bars that grow, nothing for a PR beyond its line entering with the rest.

**Layout moves** (the Workout tab only, but for the draw-in, which Home's chart also uses; `moveLayout` in `src/lib/viewTransition.ts`)

A change of what is on the page is drawn as a move from the old layout to the new one, with the browser's View Transitions. No library. The browser takes a picture of the named parts before and after and moves each from its old box to its new one over `--ww-dur-layout` with `--ww-ease`; a part that is new fades in and one that is gone fades out over `--ww-dur-base`. Everything without a name changes at once. The pictures are moved on the compositor, so this stays inside "transform and opacity only" even though a box changes size.

- **A name is live only during a move of its kind** (`ww-move-*` classes, `data-vt` on `<html>`). At rest nothing is named.
- **Step slide.** Step 1 and step 2 trade places: the old step fades out toward one side over `--ww-dur-fast`, the new one comes in `--ww-step-shift` from the other over `--ww-dur-layout`. Next comes in from the right, Back from the left. The setup line under the step moves to its new place.
- **Card to chart.** A workout card opens into its overview: the panel grows from the card's box, and the card's small graph becomes the overview's chart. The panel's content is shown at full size from the start, so no text is ever scaled. ← All workouts closes it back into the card. The other cards fade.
- **Filter reflow.** When Length, Difficulty or the side menu changes, the cards that stay move to their new places and the rest fade.
- **Course overview.** Picking a course opens its overview under the grid (a fade) and the setup line moves down to make room.
- **Draw-in** (`.ww-draw-in`). When a course is picked, its elevation profile is uncovered left to right over `--ww-dur-draw`: a cover in the panel's color shrinks away (transform only). Not when the step is shown with a course already picked. Home's training chart uses the same cover for its bars, once per page load, the first time there are bars to draw; the baseline ticks stay in view above the cover, and coming back to Home does not play it again. It is not a layout move: nothing changes place or size. The finish screen uses the same cover for its charts (see The moves).
- **Never on the Ride tab.** A layout move freezes the page for its length. The Ride tab has live numbers, so it uses none.
- **Reduced motion, or a browser without View Transitions:** the change just happens.

**What never animates:** the live numbers' digits (the Power number's color may ease to a new zone; its digits, size and place never do); the power trace (it is redrawn, never moved, and the zone color of a second never eases); the clocks (Time, Interval left, Workout left); the control row's status lines and key hint; "Not recording"; the Power tile itself (its background is never tinted); any layout on the Ride tab.

## Sound

Off by default. The app is silent unless the rider turns Interval sounds on (Profile, Your numbers).

- **Two sounds only,** both in a workout: a short beep at 3, 2 and 1 seconds left in an interval, as the countdown on screen shows that number, and a longer tone when the next interval starts or the workout ends.
- **Soft sine tones** made with Web Audio (`src/lib/sound.ts`): no files, no voice, no music. The beep is lower than the tone, so the two are told apart by ear.
- **Nothing sounds while a ride is paused,** on a free ride, on a skipped interval, or when the clock catches up after a sleeping tab.
- **Sound never carries a meaning alone.** Everything it says is already on screen (interval left, next target).
- Turning the setting on plays the tone once. It can be changed mid-ride.

## Layout

- **Top bar:** the Rullo wordmark (the solid version at `ww-wordmark-height`, 20px, in `fg`; a link to Home) and the page tabs (Home, Ride, Workout, Profile) left. With more than one rider on the browser, the current rider's name sits before the StatusPill in `ww-label`, truncated at 14 characters, a link to the Profile tab; from 1280px wide only, so the bar stays one row. With one rider it is not shown. StatusPill and the ride button right, on every page. The rightmost slot is the ride's next step: Connect (primary), then Start ride or Start workout (primary, with a secondary Disconnect to its left), then End Ride (secondary, two clicks), then Download FIT and New ride. Confirm end opens the ride's summary, the finish screen (see Ride summary), when the ride was saved; with nothing recorded the rider stays on the Ride tab. An interval change is a vertical step; a ramp slopes. None on a resistance or Grade ride. **Scale:** 0 at the bottom; the top is the larger of 120% of FTP and 110% of the highest power or target in the window, rounded up to 50 W, so it moves rarely and by a step. **States:** stale, paused or ended, the line turns `fg-subtle` with the number. ## Banned

Purple, gradients, blur, glow, shadows (except the pop-up card's, under Spacing and shape), emoji, nested panels, centered hero layouts, Inter, Geist, default shadcn or Tailwind palette colors, hex values outside `tokens.css`.
