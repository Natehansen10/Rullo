- **Server exception, Nov 21, 2026:** the app has no server, no accounts and no database, with one exception Nate made for Strava upload only: the stateless functions in `/api/strava` (shared code in `server/strava.js`). They exist to keep the Strava client secret out of the browser. Keep them stateless and small: no database, no accounts, no logging of tokens or ride data, no new dependencies. The secret is read only from `STRAVA_CLIENT_ID` / `STRAVA_CLIENT_SECRET` in Vercel and must never reach the bundle, the repo, a log or an error message. Nothing else in the app may start depending on a server, and the app must work fully without the functions. Do not use Strava data for AI; the app reads none.
- **Units:** lb, miles, feet and bpm everywhere a person reads them (UI and config). Never show kg, km or meters. SI stays inside `src/lib` (physics, course, FIT) and is converted at display.
- **Colors:** hex values live only in `src/styles/tokens.css`. The one exception is Strava's own button image in `public/strava/` (see "The Strava exception" in DESIGN.md).
- **Code reuse:** MIT or Apache-2.0 only (GPX Rider, Croissant Ride, serpentine). Keep license notices in copied files. Never copy from AGPL repos (Auuki, OpenRadlr) or unlicensed repos (ride-hud).
